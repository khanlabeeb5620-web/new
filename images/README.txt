Put your real brand images in this folder before you go live:

images/logo.png    -> your logo, shown in the top navigation and footer
                       (already included — replace with your own anytime)

Product photos are handled for you automatically by the admin panel
(/admin/) — you don't need to place those by hand. Upload them per
product and they land in:

images/products/<product-slug>/<filename>

images/placeholder.svg is a "photo coming soon" graphic the site shows
automatically whenever a product has no photo yet, or an image file
goes missing. Leave it as-is, or replace it with your own graphic
(keep the filename "placeholder.svg").

images/icons/ holds small site graphics (favicon, decorative divider)
— you can leave these as they are.
